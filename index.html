How to Use This App

Landed helps you find prospects, price the pitch, and track the close — three tools, no setup required. Here's what each one does.

Before you start

Click My Agency in the top right and fill in your name, contact info, and (optionally) a logo. This is what signs your outreach scripts and brands your proposals — you only have to do it once.

1 Outreach Scripts

Writes a ready-to-send cold email or DM pitching a prospect on a specific service.

  1. Open the Outreach Scripts tab.
  2. Choose the prospect's business type from Prospect's business type.
  3. Optionally type their name into Prospect's business name.
  4. Pick what you're pitching under Service you're pitching — Landing page, Promo video, Email management, or Social management.
  5. Choose a Tone (Direct or Casual) and a Format (Cold email or Short DM).
  6. The script writes itself on the right as you go. Click Copy, then paste it into your email or DM — swap in a real detail or two before sending.

2 Pricing Proposal

Builds a branded, priced one-pager you can send or print for a prospect.

  1. Open the Pricing Proposal tab.
  2. Enter the Prospect's business name and, optionally, a Contact name.
  3. Check the box next to each service you're pitching.
  4. Drag each service's price slider to set your price inside the market range shown.
  5. The proposal builds itself on the right. Click Print / Save as PDF to save or print it.

3 Prospect Tracker

Keeps a simple list of everyone you've reached out to, and where things stand.

  1. Open the Prospect Tracker tab.
  2. Fill in the business name, pick their type, add a note if you want, then click Add prospect.
  3. Use the Status dropdown on each row to move them from Contacted → Proposal sent → Won (or Lost).
  4. The counts at the top update automatically, so you always know where things stand at a glance.

One thing to know

Everything — your agency profile and your prospect list — is saved in this browser only. There's no account and no server, so use the same browser and device each time to keep your data.
